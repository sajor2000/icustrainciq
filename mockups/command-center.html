How this forecast was made
Each bed
Left of the arrow: life support running at 07:00. Right: new life support with a chance of 2 in 10 or more in the next 24 hours, and the bed with an arrow when leaving the ICU is 6 in 10 or more. A plus in a ring is the combined chance when no single support reaches 2 in 10.
Worsening and Watch
The chance of any new life support (IMV, vasopressor or CRRT): Worsening at 4 in 10 or more (level High), Watch at 2 in 10 or more (level Medium). Support already running is not counted again.
Not improving
On at least one life support now, a chance of leaving the ICU under 2 in 10, and no new support flagged. Worked out on this screen from the five forecasts; coming off support is not forecast.
Leaving and Steady
Leaving: 6 in 10 or more to leave the ICU for a ward or step-down. Steady: none of the above. Death never marks a bed.
Icons
Unit strain
Counts carry a range covering 8 in 10 likely outcomes (p10 to p90). The bead is the median, the solid line is what is free or what tonight's staffing covers, the dashed line is a typical day. A figure fills to its bed's chance; a solid figure is on that support now.
Status words
With tonight's capacity set: ON TRACK when the range stays at or under it, TIGHT when the range passes it, SHORT when the median passes it. Without it: USUAL, BUSY or VERY BUSY against a typical day.
