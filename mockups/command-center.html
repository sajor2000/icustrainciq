Event thresholds

An event is flagged on a bed when its chance is at or above that event's threshold: the event's circle icon then shows on the bed's card. When a new life support is at its threshold, the bed's person icon turns red and pulses (death and discharge alone do not). The standard lines are 20% (watch) and 40% (worsening), and 60% for leaving the ICU; change any line below, or press Reset to standard.

How this forecast was made
What is forecast
Five predictions for every patient, each for the next 24 h (07:00 to 07:00): starting a ventilator (IMV), starting a vasopressor, starting CRRT, leaving the ICU, and death in hospital. The unit numbers are those patients added up.
Each bed
Under NOW: life support running at 07:00. Under NEXT 24 H, each in a circle with its chance: a new life support, death in hospital or discharge from the ICU, shown when its chance is at or above that event's threshold in Settings. When a new life support or death is at its threshold, the person icon on the bed's card turns red and pulses; discharge alone does not. A bed has no status colour.

Icons
Unit strain
Counts carry a range covering 8 in 10 likely outcomes (p10 to p90). The bead is the median, the solid line is what tonight's staffing covers, the dashed line is a typical day. With machine totals set, a card's bar is the whole total: solid for in use now, lighter with the flagged beds, red when that passes the total (SHORT), amber label AT LIMIT when it is exactly the total. A figure fills to its bed's chance; a solid figure is on that support now. Hover, focus or long-press any icon, figure, bar or chip for what it means.
