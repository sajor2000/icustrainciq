ICU Lookahead in one page

CIQ x NVIDIA Local AI Hackathon, team7. For judges from NVIDIA, CIQ and VAST. Decision support only: not a medical device, not prospectively validated. The board shows staffing and machine needs, not treatments.

Category and aim. Healthcare data science, novel methods, run locally. The aim is privacy and low investment: a small ICU or smaller hospital on two desk-size machines it owns, with patient data staying on site.

What it does

At 07:00 rounds, each ICU patient who has been in the ICU at least 24 hours gets up to five predictions for the next 24 hours: leaves the ICU, starts a ventilator, a vasopressor or CRRT (kidney dialysis), dies. Six small classifiers feed the five (ward plus step-down is "leaves the ICU"). Each chance shows as a whole percentage on every screen. Patients are added up into unit counts with a likely range (8 in 10).

The model

The team calls it GEM. We trained it from scratch on MIMIC-IV-Ext-CLIF patient timelines, written as sequences of events (a lab, a drug start, a transfer) over our own vocabulary of about 1,200 tokens. No pretrained weights from Google or NVIDIA, no fine-tuning. We borrowed the layer pattern of NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B, not a copy: at size m (about 35M parameters), 16 layers, every sixth one full attention, the rest sliding-window. The plan was 3 epochs; the checkpoint packaged as the model is step 2616 of 4351, because the training runs were canceled.

From chart to prediction

  1. The patient's last 4,096 events before the cut become a list.
  2. The model reads the list once. It does not write out the next 24 hours. It keeps a summary of the patient so far: the hidden state.
  3. A probe, a small classifier, reads that summary and gives a chance for each question. Each was trained on past stays, cut at ICU arrival + 24 h, using what really happened in the next 24 hours. The probe file carries a provenance sidecar that is checked against the model before it loads.
  4. Patients are added up into unit counts.

We first tried about 100 sampled futures per patient and dropped it for one pass.

Results

Held-out AUROC is 0.77 to 0.93 across the six classifiers, on retrospective MIMIC-IV, one dataset. Which checkpoint was scored is not stated in the results file. Backtest against persistence and GB10 run times: Not ready yet.

Privacy

Real MIMIC-IV stays counts-only by default; real-ward views are allowed locally under the team's data agreement. Rooms shown by default are the open MIMIC-IV Demo ward.
